# Demo, Deck, README — FullPay (HackCache '26, PS4 "The Small Business Invoice Chase")

Lens: how six asynchronous judges, scoring from four links, experience us. Inputs read in full: `problemstatement/chase` (statement + 16-section pack), `10_build_plan.md` (Rev B–F.1), `13_reference_library.md`, `14_build_checklist.md`, `09_demo_and_judging_strategy.md`; `lld.html` grepped for `/verify`, outbox, demo clock, seed and reseed. Research and design only; no application code is proposed before kickoff.

## 0. Verdict in six lines

1. **The video is the submission.** Judges with a stack of entries watch the video first and open the repo or URL only for the entries they already like ([Ray Deck, five hackathon wins](https://sustained.substack.com/p/lessons-from-winning-five-hackathons); [Jacklyn Biggin, MLH coach: judges "often have five minutes" per project](https://dev.to/jacklyn/how-to-make-your-devpost-submissions-not-suck-37i0)). Every pack beat and every novelty beat must be *in* the 3:00, not "available on the live URL".
2. **Resolve the two scripts by merging, not choosing.** Three of the pack's eight expected beats are the *same screens* as three of our four novelty beats (demand letter = interest letter; demo date forward = the mechanism of every novelty beat; dispute-pauses-ladder = the same pause/resume machinery as promise-break). Merged, all 12 beats fit in 2:59 with the novelty beats keeping 100 of 179 seconds.
3. **Open cold on proof, not on a dashboard.** Every "Baaki" clone opens on a dashboard. We open at 0:00 on the Render Workflow run row + our Runs panel + the real email, then title, then the pack's order. This satisfies the plan's "autonomy first" (Rev B #6) and the pack's checklist order (Rev F.1 #12) at once.
4. **Captions are the pack's "one sentence on the problem it solves".** Burned in at the top band (judges may watch muted; the bottom is the CC zone — [MobileHCI video guidelines](https://mobilehci.acm.org/2024/accessibility.php)), plus the same `.srt` uploaded as closed captions, plus YouTube chapters named after the pack's beats so a checklist-driven judge can jump ([YouTube chapter rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579): ≥3 timestamps, first at 00:00, each ≥10 s).
5. **Deck = 10 slides, every number sourced on the slide**, exported to PDF in `docs/` (GitHub renders PDFs but ignores links inside them — [GitHub docs](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/working-with-files/using-files/working-with-non-code-files) — so URLs are printed as text + QR) and shared as Google Slides "anyone with the link".
6. **README = the pack's Submit section, heading for heading, plus a 2-minute judge tour at the top.** Three catches surfaced while designing the tour: the Sunday 10:00 IST run may have nothing to send if the seed's T0 stays 24 Oct and Saturday's test ticks consume the three auto-sends; pay tokens are `randomBytes` so the README's `/pay/<token>` link dies at every nightly reseed; Resend idempotency keys `outbox-<id>-<attempt>` repeat after reseed inside the 24-hour window. All three are 10–30-minute fixes (additions A6, A7).
