# Competitor simulation — PS4 "The Small Business Invoice Chase", FullPay vs the field

Written Fri 23 Oct 2026, early afternoon IST, from the pack (`problemstatement/chase`), the locked plan (`10_build_plan.md` Rev F.1), the reference library (`13_reference_library.md`) and a live GitHub / web sweep. Research only; no application code.

## 0. Headlines

1. **A real PS4 rival is already public: "Tagada"** — [Anikesh-Paul/hackcache-public](https://github.com/Anikesh-Paul/hackcache-public). It is a Render-Workflows + Render-cron build with an MSMED §16 engine that already switches to 17.25% on 7 Oct 2026, a six-rung ladder ending "Payment Demand Letter → ODR case file", promise-to-pay with instalments, a bank-transaction table with a `tds` match kind, per-visitor demo worlds (every judge gets a private seeded workspace), an in-browser `/proof` page that runs the brief's money checks, an ElevenLabs AI "reminder call" rung and Gemini reading of UPI screenshots. First commit 22 Oct 14:48 IST (about 31 h before kickoff), seven commits by 23 Oct 10:14 IST. It partially overlaps **both** FullPay novelty levers (statutory ladder + ODR, promise-aware escalation). It lacks: any hash chain or public verifier; the dated RBI series (its table has two rows — every date before 7 Oct 2026 is charged 16.5%, which is wrong for any delay before 5 Dec 2025); 0% dispute spans; the interest-first appropriation rule; sources in config; full Hindi UI (header language only so far); Postgres transactions; the pack's 3% and 40% GST rates.
2. **The mass of the field will be pack-verbatim AI builds.** The organiser's listing says coding experience is not required and AI/no-code tools are allowed ([Internshala listing](https://internshala.com/competitions/?p=12241)). A HackCache PS5 team is already shipping a Lovable "remix" ([nafisdevtale/remix-of-saheli-cycle-companion](https://github.com/nafisdevtale/remix-of-saheli-cycle-companion)) with ten commits between 22:30 and 04:57 UTC and a README claiming 13 languages and 129 tests; its README embeds "SAHELI — HACKCACHE STAGE 1: COMPLETE BUILD PROMPT", confirming the pack family. Expect many Baaki / Sharma Packaging clones with large claims and localStorage.
3. **The fintech team's enablers are weaker than they sound.** Razorpay: "Use Test Mode to test UPI payments, and Live Mode for UPI Intent and QR payments" — test-mode UPI is a mock bank page with `success@razorpay` / `failure@razorpay`, "No real money is deducted … This is a simulated transaction" ([test integration](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-gateway/android-integration/custom/test-integration/?preferred-country=IN), [test details](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payments/test-card-upi-details/)). Smart Collect is "not available to the Individuals merchant category" and needs a current/escrow account enabled via a point of contact ([Smart Collect](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/smart-collect/)). The NIC e-invoice sandbox lists enablement by taxpayer turnover tier (above ₹5 cr) with test GSTINs and no route for a developer without a GSTIN ([einv-apisandbox.nic.in](https://einv-apisandbox.nic.in/)). Their best realistic moment is "pay link → webhook → invoice auto-marked paid", which **is** judge-reproducible in test mode.
4. **Devpost and Devfolio hold nothing.** HackCache runs on the organiser's own portal (the plan's "submit via the dashboard"; the only public listing is Internshala). GitHub is the only public signal. No public repo uses the pack's "Sharma Packaging" or "Rakesh Sharma" yet; two "baaki" repos exist — one empty, created 22 Oct ([ashu6711/baaki](https://github.com/ashu6711/baaki)), one an unrelated voice udhar-ledger scaffold from 15 Oct ([Pranesh0805-S/Baaki](https://github.com/Pranesh0805-S/Baaki)).
5. **FullPay's edges that no observed rival has:** tamper-evident chain + QR `/verify` with the buyer-side "pay only to" check; the full dated RBI Bank Rate series with sources, 0% inside accepted dispute spans and interest-first appropriation (Gurpreet Singh); promise → forecast reflow → broken-promise auto-resume; the 13-week forecast with covering invoices; a letter that never threatens, in Devanagari; Postgres transactions and an idempotent tick. Eight additions below (about 3 h 45 min in total, most under 40 min) keep those edges visible and borrow Tagada's two best ideas (a live `/proof` page, judge-safe demo state).

## 2. Simulation 1 — the fast AI-builder team (pack verbatim, polished)

**Who.** One to three people on Lovable, Bolt or Cursor, running Build 1 → Build 2 → Submit as written: Baaki, Sharma Packaging, Pune; React + Vite + Tailwind; localStorage; on Vercel or Netlify within an hour; Sunday spent on a landing page, a README of claims and the video.

**Their 3 minutes.** Dashboard with the ageing bar → Today's follow-ups, one tap opens WhatsApp on a phone → the customer link disputes an invoice and the ladder pauses → bank CSV import with the TDS flag → same-state and other-state invoice side by side → a demand letter → the demo date moves forward. This is the pack's own demo script (pack lines 245–253), so a judge holding the pack's checklist ticks every box in two minutes.

**Where they beat FullPay.** *Design & UX*: Lovable-grade polish and motion on every screen; the Saheli remix shows a team redesigning its whole UI five times in one night. *Presentation*: the demo matches, beat for beat, what the pack told judges to expect. *Delivery risk*: nothing can be "down" during asynchronous judging; a static app has no cold start, no DNS, no cron.

**Where FullPay beats them.** *Technical Execution*: a localStorage prototype against Postgres, transactions, an idempotent scheduled tick, about fifty money-path tests, CI, a deployed scheduler with a run id on every event. *Innovation*: nothing beyond what the pack wrote; most one-shot builds also fail the pack's own checks (separate CGST/SGST rounding, consecutive FY numbering, re-import idempotency, complete Hindi). *Impact*: no real sends, no ODR bundle, interest as one config number. *Evidence*: their README claims cannot be checked.

**What FullPay must do.** (a) Open the video with the pack's 20-second montage (plan Rev F.1 #12) so the pack-holding judge ticks the same boxes before the novelty beats. (b) Make every claim checkable: the live `/proof` page (addition 1) and a README "claims audit" table that links each claim to a screen or a test; an AI build cannot answer "show me".

## 3. Simulation 2 — strong full-stack team: backend + WhatsApp sandbox + AI drafts

**Who.** Tagada-class or RecoverAI-class: TypeScript or FastAPI, Postgres / Appwrite / Supabase, a daily cron. WhatsApp through the Meta Cloud API test number (free; "relaxed messaging limits and don't require a payment method"; recipients added one by one with a WhatsApp confirmation code, up to five; `hello_world` or their own reviewed templates — [Get Started](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/get-started/), [Messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits), [secondary](https://help.wanotifier.com/en/article/test-phone-number-limitations-kt0ly2/)) or the Twilio sandbox (+1 415 523 8886; "only users who have joined your specific Sandbox can receive messages"; three pre-approved templates; "You can't use custom message templates with the Sandbox"; sessions expire after three days; "testing and discovery purposes only" — [Twilio sandbox](https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/sandbox)). Gemini or Groq drafts tone-graded messages; a classifier reads replies.

**Their 3 minutes.** The cron fires → a phone on camera buzzes with a WhatsApp from the business → an AI-drafted firmer message in Hinglish → the customer replies "will pay Friday" → the classifier logs a promise → the dashboard updates; Tagada would add an AI voice call with a consent line.

**Where they beat FullPay.** *Presentation*: a WhatsApp notification on a real phone is the most visceral five seconds available; AI replies and calls read as "AI-native" to a 2026 judge. *Innovation*: AI calling and screenshot-proof reading are features we chose not to build. *Technical Execution*: parity (webhooks, HMAC verification, templates).

**Where FullPay beats them.** *Honesty and reproducibility*: their sandbox reaches only their own enrolled phones; a judge can receive nothing; FullPay's email reaches anyone once the domain is verified, and judges can trigger it themselves (addition 2). *Pack conformance*: the pack prescribes wa.me ("needs no approval", pack line 212) and asks for the five templates in the README; FullPay ships both. *Compliance depth*: Meta's policy restricts "debt collection" and rejects templates "threatening a customer with legal action" (library B4); RecoverAI's "Legal Threat" tone would fail review; FullPay's footer, variable placement and category are already conformant. *Statutory depth*: the sourced RBI series, dispute spans, appropriation, a letter that is not a notice. *Evidence*: the chain and `/verify`. *Forecast*: none of them reflows on promises.

**What FullPay must do.** (a) The judge-triggered real email (addition 2) plus the outbox page with Resend message ids and the Workflow run id: "you just received what the scheduler sends". (b) Make the call rung tangible without a bot: a call-script card, a `tel:` link and a logged outcome (addition 7); keep P4.7 (AI-assisted draft badge) off the cut list so "AI" is visible while "no LLM in the money path" stays true.

## 4. Simulation 3 — fintech-savvy team: real UPI pay links (Razorpay or Cashfree test mode) + GST reconciliation

**Who.** Comfortable with gateways. Razorpay test keys (no KYC for test), Payment Links API → hosted page, `payment_link.paid` webhook flips the invoice to Paid; or Cashfree sandbox (`testsuccess@gocash`, `testfailure@gocash`; a UPI Intent Simulator APK for intent/QR flows — [Cashfree test data](https://docs.cashfree.com/docs/data-to-test-integration), [simulator](https://docs.cashfree.com/docs/cashfree-upi-intent-simulator-apk)). "GST reconciliation" in practice means uploading a GSTR-1 JSON/Excel export and matching issued invoices, or TDS / Form 26AS matching; Smart Collect virtual accounts get mocked.

**Their 3 minutes.** A reminder carries a Razorpay link → the judge-clickable hosted page → UPI → `success@razorpay` → webhook → the invoice flips to Paid with the gateway reference → "zero manual reconciliation" → a GSTR-1 mismatch report.

**Facts that cap them.** Test-mode UPI is a mock bank page with Success and Failure buttons, not a scan from a UPI app; Intent and QR are live-mode only; Smart Collect is closed to Individuals and needs a current or escrow account; the NIC sandbox has no path for a developer without a GSTIN above the threshold; so "real IRN" or "GSTR-2B API" claims imply a GSP sandbox they will not have in 48 hours.

**Where they beat FullPay.** *Technical Execution*: a real third-party integration with signed webhooks and idempotent payment capture. *Impact*: a payable link removes friction; collection rather than chasing. *Presentation*: a judge can actually pay in test mode. *Vocabulary*: a Banking & Finance track judge likes gateways.

**Where FullPay beats them.** *Pack conformance*: payments are simulated by design (pack line 46); the pack's Payment Reconciliation item is the bank-CSV matching with the six cases including TDS at exactly 1/2/10% (pack line 149); a gateway makes those cases vanish from their demo, so they skip the part the brief grades. *Everything statutory*: interest, letter, ODR bundle. *Forecast, trust layer, Hindi.* *Live-demo risk*: webhooks and test-mode quirks fail on camera; our path has no third party in it.

**What FullPay must do.** (a) Let the simulated UPI payment flow through the real matcher (addition 4) so the "pay → matched → ladder stops → forecast moves" moment exists in our video too, labelled simulated. (b) One deck line pre-empting the question: "Why not Razorpay? The brief says simulate; test mode cannot show a UPI QR scan; and a gateway hides the six matching cases the brief grades — we show them."
