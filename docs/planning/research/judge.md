# Judge lens — what moves a score when six judges score FullPay from four links, asynchronously

**Read first:** `problemstatement/chase` (official statement + pack), `10_build_plan.md` (Rev F.1), `13_reference_library.md`, `14_build_checklist.md` (as of 11:20 IST: build started early by team decision, declared in the README), `09_demo_and_judging_strategy.md`. Nothing below re-proposes what those already hold; every item is a sharpening or an addition, with the screen, the wording, the cost and where a judge sees it.

## 0. How the judging actually works for us

- **No live Q&A, no credentials handed over, no second chance.** Six judges open the GitHub repo, the live URL, a ≤3-minute video and a deck, and score Innovation, Technical Execution, Design & UX, Impact, Presentation. If the organiser uses a Devpost-style flow, each criterion is a 1–5 rating per submission with no feedback channel ([Devpost: how to judge an online hackathon](https://help.devpost.com/article/103-how-to-judge-an-online-hackathon)).
- **Judges watch the video first and then use it as the script to test the live link** ("watch the video… and then use that for testing the submission"); the video is "the first indicator of how much time was invested"; "ambiguity is a red flag"; they mark down "polished intros… a lot lighter on code", "rehashed ideas", and projects that are "extremely back-end heavy" with no UI ([Devpost: advice from 5 judges](https://info.devpost.com/blog/hackathon-judging-tips)). FullPay's plan is unusually back-end-and-law heavy: the judge-lens job is to make that depth **visible in seconds, without a password, from a phone**.
- **The organiser's evaluation-focus words** are the judges' mental checklist: practical usefulness, invoice management, payment-follow-up automation, accuracy of financial calculations, cash-flow forecasting, document generation, ease of use, technical execution, real-world impact (`problemstatement/chase`, "Evaluation Focus"). Every judge also holds the same pack demo script (dashboard → shortfall week → WhatsApp send → dispute → bank import → GST side-by-side → letter → demo date). Our video must let them tick that list even though it runs novelty-first.
- **The single biggest gap in the current plan:** P1.2 makes an unauthenticated `/` redirect to `/login` with `OWNER_PASSWORD`. A judge who opens the live URL hits a password box with no way in, bounces, and scores Technical Execution and Design & UX from the video alone. Fixing this costs 45 minutes and is the highest-leverage item in this document.

## 7. Risks a judge creates (see the structured list for prechecks)

Login wall at the live URL · six judges trampling one shared demo state · free Postgres expiring before results · pack-order mental checklist vs our novelty-first video · video/deck claims drifting from the frozen build · email evidence invisible to judges · video uploaded Private or over 3:00 · machine-sounding Hindi · judges on phones · "-Pay" read as a payments app · cold/slow live URL · unknown submission-form fields · the email-me feature as a quota or abuse vector.
